Successor to the original Fantasy Classes... which was a successor to Fantasy Tools...

Fantasy Classes is a massive project that aimed to add fantasy RPG elements to Minecraft. 
These include classes, professions, a new levelling system, new enemies & bosses, and plenty of world building.

*Battle tested by a server of 20 people!*

# Features

## Custom Leveling

Instead of replacing the core experience system, there is an added XP bar in the FantasyClasses menu that allows players to track their XP, level, and skill points.
On odd levels, players gain a Class point, while even levels grant a Profession point.
Players may spend and refund these points freely, as to make talent swapping quick and easy.
However, only one Class and 2 Professions may be chosen at a time.
Once a player has chosen those, they cannot invest in other classes or professions unless they reset their class/professions via a Tome of Decay (dropped from bosses).

## Lore

There's plenty of notes, hints, and dialogue that can be found throughout the plugin. 
Each has a color associated with it, hinting towards something larger.
While the messages are cryptic, they each push the players towards different aspects of the game.
